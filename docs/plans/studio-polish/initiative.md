## Reverb decay range follow-on
Bead `tunestretch-aq3` extends Reverb Decay to 60 seconds at its shared maximum used by the slider, persisted-state normalization, and Web Audio impulse generation. Pre-delay is the 0–250 ms DelayNode before convolution on the wet path (default 20 ms); damping is the 500–18,000 Hz low-pass filter after convolution on the wet path (default 8,000 Hz). Neither is essential to produce reverb, but each offers independent control over onset separation or tail brightness; both remain unchanged. Reverb Wet / dry defaults to 0%, so increasing it is necessary to hear the wet path.

In the browser, End selected `60.0s`, local storage retained 60 across reload, and live source playback created a 2-channel impulse of 2,880,000 samples per channel at 48,000 Hz (60 s). Home produced a 0.2 s impulse; the initial 2.4 s setting was restored. At 320px, `60.0s` kept its 6 rendered px visible slider/value gap, right edge x308, and no horizontal overflow; browser errors were empty. ## Natural playback effect tails
Goal (`tunestretch-50g`): when a non-looping source reaches its natural end, keep the existing main Web Audio effect graph connected until its reverb/delay tail finishes; an explicit Stop or component teardown must disconnect it immediately. Preserve overlapping tails across a subsequent Play without creating a fresh convolver per short clip. Leave the keyboard-trigger bus unchanged.

Decision: connect playback sources to one main effect input bus instead of creating a graph per source. A natural `onended` disconnects only the finished source. Schedule graph retirement against `AudioContext.currentTime` after pre-delay plus impulse duration and any audible delay feedback repeats (to −60 dB), so pause/suspension cannot silently consume the tail. If wet effects are off, retire immediately. Expose `TAIL` in transport and keep Stop available until cleanup; a new Play reuses the bus and cancels its pending retirement. Explicit Stop cuts active source and all main effects.

Evidence: in managed Chromium, a real 0.1 s, 48 kHz sine source with reverb wet at 100% and decay at 60 s reached source EOF, displayed `TAIL` with both Stop buttons enabled, retained its convolver without disconnection, and produced a nonzero destination-analyser RMS (0.000075 after EOF + 0.2 s; 0.000035 beyond EOF + 3.1 s). Replaying during the tail reused the convolver without disconnecting it and retained nonzero output; explicit Stop disconnected it and left zero output. With 0.2 s decay, the graph retired about 0.277 s after EOF. With both wet controls at zero, the footer moved `STOPPED` → `PLAYING` → `STOPPED` without `TAIL`, disconnected the graph, and announced “Playback finished.” A 100% wet delay produced nonzero output after EOF. Paused playback held `AudioContext.currentTime` constant for 0.9 s and did not end the source until resume; Stop during active playback did not later enter `TAIL`.

At 320 CSS px, the `TAIL` footer and Stop fit without horizontal overflow; the same held at 200% root font size, and Enter on the focused Stop button cut the tail. The wide footer was visually inspected, browser errors were empty, `npm run build` and `npm test` (18/18) passed, and the task-owned diff passed `git diff --check`. The keyboard-trigger bus was intentionally unchanged. The 60 s tail was observed beyond 3.1 s, not waited out to its final sample; the retirement calculation uses the selected 60 s decay, which produced a measured 60 s impulse buffer.

## Live effect-control tail timing
Follow-up (`tunestretch-50g`): changing wet delay feedback/time during `TAIL` must not let the original EOF timer disconnect audible echoes. Recalculate retirement from `AudioContext.currentTime` when tail-affecting reverb or delay settings change, using committed effect settings rather than the previous values of smoothly automated `AudioParam`s. Starting the renewed lifetime at the change also covers echoes still circulating if feedback rises near the old expiry. Lowering feedback or wet mix retires the graph after its shorter remaining tail; turning wet effects off retires immediately. Explicit Stop, replay, dry playback, and keyboard-trigger behavior remain unchanged.

In Chromium, a real 0.1 s sine source with 100% wet delay (0.3 s, feedback 0.35) reached `TAIL`; raising feedback to 0.85 at EOF + 0.021 s kept the convolver connected and produced nonzero destination output (RMS 0.000098) at EOF + 2.661 s, beyond the previous ~2.15 s deadline. Lowering live feedback from 0.85 to 0 retired the graph 0.384 s after the change. Raising delay time from 0.3 s to 1 s during `TAIL` kept the graph connected at EOF + 2.413 s, beyond its old deadline. Turning wet mix off while `TAIL` disconnected the graph within 0.016 s. With a 0.05 s delay, increasing feedback from 0.35 to 0.85 retained the tail beyond the old ~0.4 s deadline and automatically retired the graph at EOF + 2.243 s. The live-tail footer and enabled Stop fit at 320 CSS px without horizontal overflow; keyboard Enter cut the tail, and browser errors were empty. `npm run build`, `npm test` (18/18), and task-owned `git diff --check` passed.
